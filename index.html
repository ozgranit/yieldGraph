Buy vs Rent Investment Calculator

Historical 30 year Growth rates: SPY 8.81%, Housing 5.4%, Rent 2.0%

🏠 Home Buyer
📘 How the Buyer Scenario Works:
  • You put down your Initial Investment as a down payment
  • The remaining amount (plus fees) becomes your mortgage
  • You pay a fixed monthly mortgage for 30 years
  • Any income left after the mortgage payment gets invested in S&P 500
  • Your net worth = House Value - Remaining Mortgage + Stock Portfolio
  • The house appreciates at the Housing Yield rate
📈 Renter
📘 How the Renter Scenario Works:
  • You invest your full Initial Investment in S&P 500
  • You pay monthly rent (which grows annually)
  • Any income left after rent gets invested in S&P 500
  • Your net worth = Total Stock Portfolio
  • Your portfolio grows at the Expected S&P Yield rate
  • No mortgage, no property maintenance, full liquidity
